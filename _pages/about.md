## About Me

I am a **Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow at Cornell University**. My research focuses on **trustworthy and explainable AI**: understanding why models make decisions, when and how they fail, and how to make their predictions more interpretable, robust, and reliable.

My methodological work covers **feature attribution, counterfactual explanations, adversarial robustness, formal verification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation**. My explainable AI research has been published in **IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)**.

I also apply **computer vision, foundation models, and geospatial AI** to industrial fault diagnosis, urban energy systems, and scientific discovery. I received my Ph.D. from **Zhejiang University** in 2023 and was previously a Research Fellow at **Nanyang Technological University**.

## Research Directions

- **Trustworthy & Explainable AI:** Feature attribution, counterfactual explanations, adversarial robustness, and formal verification.
- **Industrial AI & Smart Manufacturing:** Anomaly detection, process monitoring, and explainable fault diagnosis.
- **AI for Energy & Urban Systems:** Geospatial AI, rooftop photovoltaics, remote sensing, and urban digital infrastructure.
- **Computer Vision & AI for Science:** Foundation models, monocular depth estimation, and 3D plant reconstruction.
