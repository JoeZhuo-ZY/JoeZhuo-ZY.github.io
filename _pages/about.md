<section class="home-section" id="about" aria-labelledby="about-title">
  <h1 id="about-title">About Me</h1>
  <div class="about-grid">
    <div class="about-copy">
      <p class="about-role">Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow · Cornell University</p>
      <p>I specialize in <strong>trustworthy and explainable AI</strong>, studying why models make certain decisions, when and how they fail, and how to make their predictions more interpretable, robust, and reliable.</p>
      <p>My research spans <strong>feature attribution, model interpretation, adversarial robustness, formal verification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation</strong>. My methodological work on explainable AI has been published in <strong>IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)</strong>.</p>
      <p>I also apply <strong>computer vision, foundation models, and geospatial AI</strong> to challenges in smart manufacturing, urban energy, and scientific discovery. I received my Ph.D. from <strong>Zhejiang University</strong> in 2023 and previously worked as a Research Fellow at <strong>Nanyang Technological University</strong>, including a multi-robot planning collaboration with Continental.</p>

<section class="home-section" id="research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <h2 id="research-title">Research Directions</h2>
  <div class="research-grid">
    <article class="research-item">
      <h3>Trustworthy &amp; Explainable AI</h3>
      <p>Feature attribution, counterfactual explanations, adversarial robustness, and formal verification of learning-based systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-item">
      <h3>Industrial AI &amp; Smart Manufacturing</h3>
      <p>Anomaly detection, process monitoring, explainable fault diagnosis, and reliable machine learning for manufacturing.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-item">
      <h3>AI for Energy &amp; Urban Systems</h3>
      <p>Remote sensing and geospatial AI for rooftop photovoltaics, urban infrastructure, and resource-intensive digital systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-item">
      <h3>Computer Vision &amp; AI for Science</h3>
      <p>Foundation models, monocular depth estimation, and 3D reconstruction for scientific imaging and plant phenotyping.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
