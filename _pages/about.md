**Trustworthy and Explainable AI**

I am a **Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow at Cornell University**. My research centers on **trustworthy and explainable AI**: understanding why models make decisions, identifying when their predictions can fail, and developing methods that make their behavior more interpretable and reliable.

I develop methods for feature attribution and model interpretation, adversarial robustness, formal verification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation. My explainability research includes **IG2** and **ABIGX**, published in **IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)**. These studies connect general questions about model behavior with the practical need to interpret and diagnose learning-based systems.

**Manufacturing, energy, and scientific discovery provide application settings for this research.** In manufacturing, I study explainable fault diagnosis and the reliability of data-driven process models. At Cornell, I apply computer vision, foundation models, and geospatial analysis to rooftop photovoltaic (PV) deployment, and develop single-image 3D reconstruction for plant science. Across these applications, I aim to connect model outputs with evidence that domain experts can interpret and validate, while making uncertainty and limitations visible.

I received my Ph.D. from **Zhejiang University** in 2023, where my doctoral research focused on explainable AI and reliable machine learning. Before joining Cornell, I was a Research Fellow at **Nanyang Technological University**, working with Continental on multi-robot planning and the robustness of multi-agent learning systems.

## Research Interests

- **Explainable AI:** Feature attribution, model interpretation, counterfactual explanations, and explainable diagnosis; connecting explanations to model behavior and domain knowledge.
- **Trustworthy machine learning:** Adversarial robustness, formal verification, probabilistic modeling, and evaluation under data scarcity, perturbations, and distribution shifts.
- **Scientific perception and agentic AI:** Computer vision, multimodal foundation models, single-view 3D reconstruction, and workflows that coordinate models with specialized analytical tools.
- **Applications in manufacturing, energy, and science:** Explainable industrial fault diagnosis; rooftop PV mapping and longitudinal deployment analysis; interpretable plant phenotyping and reconstruction.
