<section class="section" id="about" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <div class="site-container">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-index">01 / Introduction</div>
      <div>
        <h2 class="section-heading" id="about-heading">About me</h2>
        <p class="section-lede">A research agenda centered on trustworthy AI—and strengthened by real engineering and scientific problems.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="section-body about-layout">
      <div class="about-copy">
        <p>I am a <strong>Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow at Cornell University</strong>, specializing in <strong>trustworthy and explainable artificial intelligence</strong>. My research focuses on understanding why AI models make decisions, when and how their predictions fail, and how to make their behavior more interpretable, robust, and reliable.</p>
        <p>My methodological work spans <strong>explainable AI, feature attribution, adversarial robustness, formal verification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation</strong>. I develop ways to interpret complex model behavior, identify vulnerabilities, and improve the reliability of learning-based systems. My work on explainable AI has appeared in <strong>IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)</strong>.</p>
        <p>Beyond foundational methods, I work on <strong>industrial AI and smart manufacturing</strong>, and apply <strong>computer vision, foundation models, and geospatial analysis</strong> to challenges in urban energy infrastructure and scientific discovery. Across these settings, I aim to connect model outputs with evidence that scientists and engineers can understand, test, and act upon.</p>
      </div>
      <aside class="about-aside">
        <span class="aside-label">Research philosophy</span>
        <p>Build AI systems that we can not only use, but also <em>understand, test, and trust.</em></p>
      </aside>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
